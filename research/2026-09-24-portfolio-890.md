## Key numbers (facts vs. my estimates)

**Celestica (CLS), ~$350–368**
- Fact: Q2'26 revenue $4.70B (+62% YoY), adj. EPS $2.54 (+83%). FY26 guide raised to $20.5B / $11.30 adj. EPS.
- Fact: Management says 2027 revenue growth will *accelerate* past 2026's 65%, with EPS growing faster than revenue.
- Estimate (mine): that guidance implies 2027 EPS of at least ~$18.6, so the stock is ≤ ~19–20x 2027 EPS. It is ~24% below its June high.
- Catalyst: Q3 results in late October. Also 1.6T CPO switch program and Google TPU / AMD Helios ramps in 2027.
- Risk: top 3 customers = 63% of revenue. A hyperscaler capex cut would hit it directly.

**Credo (CRDO), ~$190, ~$36B market cap**
- Fact: Q1 FY27 revenue $479M (+115% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $1.20. Q2 guide $525–535M. FY27 growth guided above 85%, with optical revenue above $600M.
- Fact: The stock fell 20% after earnings because GAAP gross margin fell to 64.5% (from 68.2%), opex rose and inventory went up.
- Estimate: consensus FY27 EPS of ~$6.23 puts it at ~30x forward earnings with 85%+ growth.
- Catalyst: Q2 FY27 results in early December (gross margin recovery and optical ramp).
- Risk: three end customers were 32%, 33% and 19% of FY26 revenue.

**Micron (MU), ~$1,096**
- Fact: FQ3 revenue $41.5B. FQ4 guide $50B ±$1B and EPS $31 ±$1. Reports 30 Sep 2026. HBM capacity largely booked through 2027 and into 2028.
- Estimate: FY27 consensus EPS of ~$156 puts it at ~7x forward earnings. BofA estimates buybacks could reach up to ~$31.7B in FY27.
- Risk: memory is cyclical. FY27 capex above $45B across the industry could flood supply in 2028. Cyclical stocks look cheapest on P/E right at the peak.
