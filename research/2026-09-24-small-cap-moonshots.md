# Small-Cap "Moonshot" Screen (24 Sep 2026)

Hypothetical research output, not personal financial advice. Prices are 16–23 Sep 2026.

## The pattern real multibaggers shared
Examples from 2023–26:
- Dave went from about $5–10 in 2023 to about $360–430.
- Sterling Infrastructure, Credo and Aehr all ran hard.
- Eton's market cap went from $0.72B to $1.63B in three months.

What they had in common:
1. A small starting value (under ~$1–2B).
2. Revenue growth above 50%, often accelerating.
3. The first move into profit or positive EBITDA.
4. Little or no heavy dilution.
5. A real niche or moat in a large market.
6. A visible catalyst.
